Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Studymon is a study game made by an independent developer ("we"). This page explains what the app sends off your phone, who receives it, and what is kept. In short: Studymon has no accounts of its own, shows no ads, has no analytics, and our server keeps nothing you upload.

What stays on your phone

Kuiz, your worlds, questions, answers, progress, crystals and settings are saved in a file on your phone. You can copy that file yourself with Settings → Export save and bring it back with Import save.

Your copy in your Google account

On Android, when you are signed in to Google Play Games, Studymon also keeps a copy of that save file in Google Play Games Saved Games. Google stores it in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. It counts against your Drive storage, not ours, and we cannot read it from anywhere except the app on your phone. It lets you get your progress back on a new phone. Studymon shows your Play Games name in Settings and does not use it for anything else. Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

If you do not sign in to Google Play Games, nothing is copied and your progress stays only on your phone.

What is sent to make and grade questions

Only after you agree in the app, and only when you use these features, Studymon sends:

  • the reviewer text you paste, or the file you pick (PDF, Word, PowerPoint or a photo), with the world's name, when you ask for questions;
  • a world's saved reviewer text and its current questions, when you ask for more questions;
  • the answers you type in battles that the game cannot check by itself (essays, and Identification answers that are close but not exact), with the question and its answer key.

These go over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our study server. The server sends them on to Gemma, an open AI model made by Google, running on Cloudflare Workers AI, and sends the questions or the grade back to your phone. The server does not store them and forgets them as soon as it has replied. Cloudflare says it does not use content sent to Workers AI to train AI models or to improve its services (Cloudflare Workers AI privacy).

You can withdraw your agreement at any time by unticking Let the AI read my reviewers and answers in Settings → Privacy & data. Studymon then sends nothing more. It grades answers on your phone, and you can still add questions yourself in the World Builder.

Please do not upload material that contains other people's personal information.

Reports

If you tap Report on a question or on feedback, the text of that question or feedback is sent to our server and written to its log, so we can find and fix bad AI output.

Server logs and limits

Our server runs on Render. Its logs record each request's time, route and result, and may include the network (IP) address it came from. To share the free AI allowance fairly, the server also counts each address's requests for the current day. That count is kept only in memory and cleared every day. Render deletes the logs after about 7 days. We do not use these addresses to find your location or to identify you.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, passwords or email addresses of our own.
  • No advertising, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data. The only services that receive it are the ones named on this page: Cloudflare and Render, which work for us, and Google, if you use Google Play Games.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete my data erases the save on your phone and, if you are signed in, the copy in Google Play Games. Uninstalling the app also removes the save from the phone. Our server has nothing to delete, apart from logs that expire on their own as described above.

Age

Studymon is meant for people aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and the app shows the current version from Settings. For questions or privacy requests, email the developer address shown on Studymon's Google Play listing, or see Support.

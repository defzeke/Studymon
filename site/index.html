Studymon

Raise Kuiz, a small study companion, by looking after it at home. Then turn your own reviewer notes, PDFs, photos or slides into worlds to explore, with Challenge Houses, Gyms and a Champion to beat by answering questions about what you are studying.

Studymon is free, has no ads, and is meant for students and anyone 13 or older.

Help and support

Moving to a new phone

On Android, sign in to Google Play Games, and your save is copied to your Google account. On the new phone, install Studymon and sign in to the same Google Play Games account. Your progress comes back on its own. If both phones have progress, Studymon asks which one to keep.

Without Google Play Games, use Settings → Export save on the old phone, move the file, then Settings → Import save on the new one.

"The AI has used today's free allowance"

Every player shares one free daily AI allowance. When it runs out, Studymon writes simpler questions from your reviewer and grades answers on your phone. The allowance resets every day at 00:00 UTC.

The first upload takes a while

The study server sleeps when nobody uses it and takes up to a minute to wake. After that, it is faster.

A question or grade is wrong

Tap Report next to it in the World Builder or in a battle's Review answers. Reports help fix the AI's prompts. You can also change a world's questions with Edit questions in the Worlds list.

Turning the AI off

In Settings → Privacy & data, untick Let the AI read my reviewers and answers. Nothing more is sent, and answers are graded on your phone.

Deleting everything

Settings → Delete my data erases your save on the phone and its copy in Google Play Games. It cannot be undone.

Contact

Email the developer address shown on Studymon's Google Play listing. You can also report a bug on GitHub, but those reports are public, so do not post personal information there.

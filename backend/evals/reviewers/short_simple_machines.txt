Simple machines make work easier by changing the size or direction of a force. A lever is a rigid bar that turns on a fixed point called the fulcrum. A pulley is a wheel with a rope that changes the direction of a pulling force. An inclined plane is a sloped surface, like a ramp, that lets you raise a load with less force over a longer distance. A wedge is two inclined planes joined together, such as an axe blade. A screw is an inclined plane wrapped around a cylinder. Mechanical advantage is the output force divided by the input force.
